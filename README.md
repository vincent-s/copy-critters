# Copy Critters

A memory sequence game for kids aged 6–10. Watch the colour blobs light up and sing their note, then tap them back in the same order. Each level adds one more step.

**Play it:** https://vincent-s.github.io/copy-critters/

## How to play

1. Choose **1 Player** or **2 Players**.
2. Watch and listen as the blobs light up one at a time.
3. Tap them in the same order. Keys 1–6 work too.

- Level 1 starts with 2 steps. Each level adds one and plays a little faster.
- At level 5, Purple and Orange join the board.
- Each player has 3 hearts and wins a bonus heart every 5 levels.

## Two players

Players take turns, and each one copies their own parade. A player who runs out of hearts is out, and the other keeps going. Whoever finishes the most levels wins.

## High scores

Your score is the number of levels you finished. The top 10 scores are kept on the high score board. If you make the board, you can type your name. Scores are saved in the browser, so they stay after you close the game, but each device and browser keeps its own board.

## Run locally

It's a single file with no build step. Open `index.html` in any modern browser.
